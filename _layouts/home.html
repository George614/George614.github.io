I'm Zhizhuo Yang (杨智卓), a machine learning researcher interested in how agents perceive, act and learn. Today those agents are language models. At WRITER I own LLM post-training end to end, as the primary builder of the agentic reinforcement learning stack: data synthesis, verification and rewards, and training a 744-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with the production agent harness as its environment. I also built the synthetic tool-use data pipeline behind Palmyra x6, WRITER's shipped agentic model.

I came to machine learning through optics and virtual reality, with degrees from Beijing Jiaotong University and Beijing Institute of Technology. For my Ph.D. at Rochester Institute of Technology, advised by Dr. Alexander G. Ororbia II and Dr. Reynold Bailey, I started with deep learning for eye tracking and for how the eyes and head move together, and from 2020 I worked on active inference, which became my dissertation on perception, action and learning. Internships at Facebook and Meta took me into rendering for VR headsets and sensor fusion for glove tracking.

I have mentored students since my master's, and at WRITER I supervised an intern working on diffusion language models. Away from work I love music and movies, play basketball and badminton, travel, and hike with a camera. I also play a little guitar.

The way here

From optics and virtual reality, through eye tracking and active inference, to training language models that act.
