At WRITER I own LLM post-training end to end, as the primary builder of the agentic reinforcement learning stack: data synthesis, verification and rewards, and training a 744-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with the production agent harness as its environment.

Before that I earned a Ph.D. at Rochester Institute of Technology. I started with eye tracking and gaze, and from 2020 I worked on active inference, which became my dissertation on perception, action and learning.
