Projects and papers

  • Reinforcement learning for a 744B-parameter agent

    End-to-end GRPO training across 8-node GPU clusters, with the production agent as the environment. WRITER, 2025 to 2026

  • Palmyra x6: an agentic, tool-use model

    WRITER's shipped agentic model, post-trained on data from a synthetic tool-use pipeline I built. It has the top BFCL Core score (0.785) among the compared models. Technical report, arXiv 2026

  • Denoising Blocks, Not Tokens: Efficient Compressed Continuous Diffusion with Branching Token Realization

    Under review at ICLR 2027

  • When Are Errors Evidence? Deciding Whether to Revise, What to Keep, and How to Commit in Diffusion Language Models

    Under review at ACL ARR October 2026

  • Self-evolving agentic AI systems

    Agents that learn from their own mistakes, through planning with tree search, self-correction and continual fine-tuning. WRITER, 2025

  • Differential Transformer with low-rank adapters

    Making the differential Transformer cheaper to train and better at long context, with low-rank adapters and hyper-attention. WRITER, 2025

  • SR-AIF: sparse-reward robotics from pixels

    An active inference agent that learns its own goal states, solving sparse-reward robot manipulation from camera images. ICRA 2025

  • A neural active inference model of perceptual-motor learning

    A neural active inference agent learns to intercept a target that changes speed and, like people do, speeds up early in anticipation of the change. Frontiers in Computational Neuroscience, 2023

  • RIT-Eyes

    A Blender platform that renders labeled near-eye images from a detailed eye model, for training eye segmentation networks and testing how they generalize. ACM SAP 2020

  • Gaze-in-Wild

    A labeled dataset of eye and head movements during everyday tasks, with baseline classifiers for fixations, pursuits and saccades while the head moves freely. Scientific Reports, 2020

  • Passive haptics based mixed reality system

    A mixed reality system in which people handle real, tracked objects with bare hands while seeing virtual counterparts, calibrated for accuracy and tested for teaching geography. ISMAR 2016 and VRCAI 2016
