Meow Translator

iPhone app

Meow Translator listens to your cat’s meows, analyzes the sound and names the emotion it hears. Past readings are kept in the History tab, and recordings can be saved automatically.

Main results

  • Listens to a cat's meows and reports the emotion it hears: happy, playful, calm or curious.
  • Keeps past readings in a History tab.
  • Offers notifications, haptic feedback and automatic saving of recordings.
